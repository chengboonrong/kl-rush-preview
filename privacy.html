KL Rush privacy

KL Rush is a free browser game. You don't need an account to play, and it has no ads and sets no cookies. From version 0.4.20 the public site counts visits with cookie-free analytics (see below), and it has no advertising or cross-site tracking.

KL Rush: Zombie Night, at kl-rush-zombie-night.vercel.app, is built from the same game, so this page covers it too, except that it doesn't count visits. Its saves are separate from KL Rush's.

Visit counts (from version 0.4.20)

The public site (kl-rush.vercel.app) uses Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. It records page views and, for each visit, the country, device type, browser, operating system and the site that referred you. It sets no cookies, does not follow you across other sites, and stores no name, account or saved progress. Vercel handles connection details such as IP addresses to produce the counts, under its privacy policy. The script loads only on kl-rush.vercel.app, after the loading screen. Browsers or extensions that block trackers will usually block it, and then nothing is counted.

Error reports (from version 0.2)

To find and fix problems on devices the developer doesn't own, the game can send anonymous error reports. They are switched on by default and you can turn them off at any time in Settings → Error reports.

A report can contain these technical diagnostics:

  • the game version (for example 0.2.0-preview);
  • browser name and device type (for example "Safari · iOS"), the graphics renderer (WebGPU or WebGL2), graphics quality, driving physics and window size;
  • how long each loading step took;
  • for errors: the error message and the part of the game's code where it happened (stack trace);
  • whether you were on the title screen or playing;
  • graphics-loss details, loading stage and whether the page was visible or in the background;
  • browser and page context collected by Sentry, such as the page address and browser information.

The game also sends a report if loading takes longer than 20 seconds, once per version for that browser.

The game does not attach your name, email, account, location, saved progress or input fields to these reports. Each visit sends at most three error or slow-load reports, plus at most two benchmark reports that you separately choose to send. Consecutive duplicate errors are filtered.

Reports are sent to the developer's project on Sentry, an error-monitoring service, in Sentry's EU data region. Access and retention follow the project's Sentry settings.

The game disables Sentry's default personal-data collection and sends no user identifiers. Sentry and the hosting provider (Vercel) handle connection details such as IP addresses to receive and deliver data, under their own privacy policies (Sentry, Vercel).

Benchmark results (from version 0.4)

Settings → Benchmark measures how smoothly the game runs on your device. Running a benchmark does not upload its result automatically.

  • Send result anonymously sends technical benchmark diagnostics to Sentry: the score and recommendation, frame rates and frame times, CPU and physics timings, per-scene results, draw calls, loading times, memory counts, graphics-chip name, renderer and graphics settings, screen size, browser and system names, and game version. It also includes the game and browser context described above. This action is unavailable while Error reports is off, and you can send at most two results per visit. The developer uses these results to see how the game runs on real devices.
  • Share in Feedback opens GitHub's feedback form with the displayed result filled in. GitHub receives that prefilled text when the form opens, but an issue becomes public only if you submit it. This is a separate action from sending a result to Sentry.
  • Copy result copies the displayed result to your clipboard.

Screenshots (from version 0.6)

Screenshots you take with K, the phone's Camera app or photo mode stay on your device. The Gallery keeps your last 30 in your browser's storage for this site, and nothing is uploaded. Download saves a file, and Share and Copy hand the picture to your device's own share sheet or clipboard, only when you choose them. Clearing the site's data deletes the Gallery.

Data on your device

Your progress and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on your device, as is your choice of graphics engine (Settings → Graphics engine). Saved progress stays on your device unless you use cloud save, described below. Diagnostic reports include the settings described above. Export backup in Settings creates a file only when you choose to. Clearing your browser's site data deletes your progress on that device.

Google sign-in and cloud save (optional)

Signing in is optional, and you can play without it. It isn't offered on Zombie Night, in the desktop apps, in TV mode or in the iPhone home-screen app.

If you sign in with Google, the game keeps a copy of your save in your own Google Drive so you can carry on from another device. KL Rush has no server for this: your browser talks to Google directly, and the developer never sees your save or your Google account details.

The save is one file with the same contents as Export backup, kept in a hidden folder in your Drive that only KL Rush can open. The game asks for permission to use that folder and nothing else in your Drive, so it can't read your other files.

Sign-in happens on Google's own page, and the game never sees your password. Google then gives the game your account ID, name, email address and a link to your profile picture. The game keeps these in your browser's local storage to show who is signed in and to tell your save from another account's, along with the Drive file's ID, the time of the last sync and a fingerprint of your progress that it uses to spot changes. The fingerprint is also stored with the file in your Drive. Settings loads your picture from that link. None of this is sent to the developer, and the game doesn't add it to error reports. The short-lived pass Google gives the game stops working when it expires, and it stays in your browser's session storage until you close the tab or sign out.

Google stores the file and runs the sign-in, under its privacy policy. Where sign-in is offered, the game loads Google's script from Google's servers as soon as the sign-in button appears, so Google receives connection details such as your IP address even if you never tap it. Google's sign-in page uses Google's own cookies.

Delete cloud save in Settings deletes the file from your Drive and signs you out on that device. The save on your device stays. Sign out forgets your account on that device and leaves the file in your Drive. To withdraw the game's permission, remove KL Rush from your Google Account's connected apps.

KL Rush's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Feedback and donations

  • The Feedback button opens a form on GitHub. Anything you post there is public and covered by GitHub's privacy statement.
  • The Support button opens Ko-fi. If you donate, Ko-fi and its payment providers handle that under their own policies. The game itself receives nothing from them.
  • Settings → Report a translation opens a form on GitHub filled in with your language, the game version and the keys of the lines you were looking at. Like any GitHub issue, it becomes public only if you submit it.
