# Roadmap

KL Rush grows in small public steps. Each version ships when it is ready, not on a fixed date. The order may change based on your feedback.

| Version | Focus | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.1 Preview | Public preview | The whole game playable; feedback and support buttons; release notes |
| 0.2 Preview | Foundations | Done: crash reports, save backup, key remapping, subtitle size, reduced motion, colour-blind friendly markers, instant graphics settings, gamepad menus, an iPhone loading fix (about 5 s on an iPhone 15 Plus) |
| 0.3 Preview | Driving feel | Done: a skyline loading screen, real vehicle physics to try in Settings (suspension, kerbs, rollovers, real crash damage, gears), a crash camera with slow motion, engine, tyre and crash sounds. Next: your feedback decides whether the new physics becomes the default |
| 0.4 Preview | Living city | Done: traffic that reacts, police roadblocks, dawn, lunch and pasar malam crowds, police and traffic on real physics (with the new physics), Simplified Chinese preview, a benchmark, half the draw calls. Since 0.4.0: Malay story voices and street chatter, food culture, Petaling Street's roof, local cars, a detailed Rimba, a zoomable map, simpler touch controls, mission checkpoints and retry, and jobs that open through the story. Next: your feedback and benchmark results |
| 0.5 Beta | Content complete | Done: 14 story missions, a driving tutorial, real motorbike handling, Liga Malam night races, fuel and petrol stations, the MyCard and licences with roadside checks, a Tolak 'n Go card and three rail lines, a flyover and diagonal streets, a graffiti lane and a rooftop, controller and touch prompts, first-time tips. **Saves always carry over from here on.** |
| 0.6 Beta | The end of the story, a bigger city | Done: Arc 3 (missions 15 to 22), three side jobs and a completion screen, a 13 × 13 map with Malaysian street details, AKLEH and chain shops, screenshots and photo mode, controllers and mice, mission 1 voiced, Chinese for every line, install as an app, more accessibility |
| 0.7 Beta | KLIA and Subang | Done: planes over KL, KLIA Terminal 1 and 2 by the KLIA Ekspreso, the south expressway with a toll plaza and an R&R, the Federal Highway to Subang's Skypark Terminal, airport jobs, Bomba and ambulance jobs, walk-in shops, two businesses to buy into, six stranger encounters, touch controls that fit phones and tablets. 0.7.1: the whole story voiced in Malay and Manglish |
| 0.8 Beta | Under the city, then Sepang | Done: the SMART Tunnel, which closes for flooding in heavy rain, and five more stranger encounters (eleven in all). 0.8.1: Cantonese voices. 0.8.2–0.8.6: fewer stutters, fixes from your reports, and music of the game's own. Next: formula racing and a race weekend at Sepang |
| 0.9 Beta | Genting | Done: Genting Highlands is open (0.9.7), with the mountain road, Awana and its cable car, and the hilltop resort. Also story cutscenes, Play on TV, the desktop app and cooler phones |
| 1.0 RC 1 | Racing and combat | Done: formula racing, the race weekend, melee and the handgun opened to everyone. KL Rush then went back to beta numbers, because 1.0 is still a long way off |
| **0.10 Beta** (now) | Road-aware GPS | GPS that follows the roads, a new hall at Pasar Seni, an optional natural walk and run on computers. 0.10.1: street games, roadworks on Raja Laut, Hospital Kuala Lumpur and cloud save with Google |
| 0.11 Beta | Better everything | Buildings, cars, characters and streets brought up to the standard of the best ones, and more rebuilt cars |
| 0.12 Beta | Utara | Penang, Alor Setar and Langkawi |
| 0.13 Beta | Selatan | Melaka and Johor Bahru |
| 0.14 and 0.15 Beta | Borneo | Kuching (Sarawak), then Kota Kinabalu (Sabah) |
| 0.16 Beta | Sayap | Fly the plane yourself |
| Later | Online | Private rooms for up to 8 friends. On hold for now |
| 1.0 | Launch | Not planned yet |

Want something on this list sooner, or something that isn't here? [Tell us](https://github.com/chengboonrong/kl-rush-preview/issues/new?template=feedback.yml).
