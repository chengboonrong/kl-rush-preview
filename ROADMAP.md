# Roadmap to 1.0

KL Rush grows in small public steps. Each version ships when it is ready, not on a fixed date. The order may change based on your feedback.

| Version | Focus | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.1 Preview | Public preview | The whole game playable; feedback and support buttons; release notes |
| 0.2 Preview | Foundations | Done: crash reports, save backup, key remapping, subtitle size, reduced motion, colour-blind friendly markers, instant graphics settings, gamepad menus, an iPhone loading fix (about 5 s on an iPhone 15 Plus) |
| 0.3 Preview | Driving feel | Done: a skyline loading screen, real vehicle physics to try in Settings (suspension, kerbs, rollovers, real crash damage, gears), a crash camera with slow motion, engine, tyre and crash sounds. Next: your feedback decides whether the new physics becomes the default |
| 0.4 Preview | Living city | Done: traffic that reacts, police roadblocks, dawn, lunch and pasar malam crowds, police and traffic on real physics (with the new physics), Simplified Chinese preview, a benchmark, half the draw calls. Since 0.4.0: Malay story voices and street chatter, food culture, Petaling Street's roof, local cars, a detailed Rimba, a zoomable map, simpler touch controls, mission checkpoints and retry, and jobs that open through the story. Next: your feedback and benchmark results |
| **0.5 Beta** (now) | Content complete | Done: 14 story missions, a driving tutorial, real motorbike handling, Liga Malam night races, fuel and petrol stations, the MyCard and licences with roadside checks, a Tolak 'n Go card and three rail lines, a flyover and diagonal streets, a graffiti lane and a rooftop, controller and touch prompts, first-time tips. **Saves always carry over from here on.** |
| 0.6–0.8 Beta | More story, polish and reach | The rest of the story (Arc 3), Chinese translation review, install as an app, offline play, more accessibility, smoother phones |
| 0.9 Beta / 1.0 RC | Final testing | Bug fixing only |
| **1.0** | Launch | After 1.0: free updates with fixes and new content |

Want something on this list sooner, or something that isn't here? [Tell us](https://github.com/chengboonrong/kl-rush-preview/issues/new?template=feedback.yml).
