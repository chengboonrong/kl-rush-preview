Bawah tanahNew in 0.8: under the city, and five more strangers

A tunnel modelled on the SMART Tunnel runs under KL from Jalan Syed Putra to Jalan Jelatek: one tube, two lanes each way, 10 m down. Cars and taxis only, as in the real one. In heavy rain it closes for flooding: the phone, the radio and the signs warn you, the gates shut once nobody is inside, and it reopens after the rain.

Five more "?" marks open as the story goes on: a tourist's car towed off Bukit Bintang, a student late for her exam, a kapcai thief in Petaling Street's market, a busker's stolen amplifier and Uncle Raj's lost cat. The completion screen counts eleven. Every line of the story is spoken in Malay and Manglish, and since 0.8.1 in Cantonese too.

Keluar KLNew in 0.7: out of the city

Open the phone's Travel app and pick a way out. The KLIA Ekspreso leaves KL Sentral for Terminal 1, then Terminal 2. The south expressway runs between oil palm estates to a toll plaza, an R&R and the KLIA exit. The Federal Highway heads west to Subang's Skypark Terminal, where turboprops land. Your car, fuel, passengers, money and wanted level come with you.

At KLIA, an airside pass opens four jobs: baggage tug, fuel bowser, pushback and the airport limousine. Back in KL, the Bomba Sukarela and the ambulance take you on, every chain shop opens its doors (nothing is for sale), and planes cross the sky.

Sehari di KLOne day in the city, by the game clock

The game is real Kuala Lumpur at phone size: real landmarks and streets, a new look made in Blender, muted walls and roads, and strong colour only where KL has it. A day here takes 24 real minutes, one game minute every second. This is what that day looks like in 0.8.

16:30A new game starts

You land at Pasar Seni

No rain until your first mission is done. In front of you is a 13 × 13 grid of city blocks, with new districts in Titiwangsa, Setapak, Ampang, Mid Valley, Bangsar South, Bangsar and along the Federal Highway. The Sungai Klang runs through it, with left-hand traffic on every road.

Jalan P. Ramlee, Jalan Kuching and Jalan Bukit Bintang have flyovers, and AKLEH runs over Jalan Ampang. Malaysian road signs, blue street names, black-and-white kerbs, monsoon drains, footbridges, bus stops and a motorcycle lane make the streets read like KL.

PetangAfternoon, often wet

Fill up, get your papers in order

Afternoon thunderstorms roll in and leave the roads shining. On dry days, jerebu haze can settle over the city instead; switch it off in Settings. Motorbikes now run on real physics: they lean into corners and throw you off in a crash.

SenjaDusk

Ride the rails

Every monorail stop on the map, plus the Kelana Jaya and Kajang lines underground. Fares go by distance.

New in 0.5.7: ride the underground trains through the tunnel to your stop, and walk down station entrances built like KL's, each with a lit name banner. 0.5.6 rebuilt all 11 underground stations from photos.

Pay with a Tolak 'n Go card or cash at three Kedai 24 Jam stores. The courier job sends you between stations with parcels.

18:30Pasar malam opens

Petaling Street lights up

The pasar malam runs until about 23:30 under the long roof and its sixty-four red lanterns. Off to the side, the new graffiti lane: original wall art, preset stamps to leave your mark, and the sixteenth stray cat.

After six, Jalan Alor has satay smoke and plastic stools, and a durian stall that serves an opened fruit for RM 6. Every dish the stalls serve can be turned and zoomed in 3D from the phone's Food journal: all seventeen are on the menu below.

Around KL, 38 shopfronts from six parody chains open and close on the game clock; the Shops layer and GPS find the ones that are open. Since 0.7 you can walk in, down the aisles, until the shutter comes down.

Press K or use the phone's Camera app for a screenshot. Photo mode freezes the game so you can frame it, change the look and hide the player.

Jom makanSeventeen dishes, every one in 3D

The prices are the game's own, in ringgit. Drinks come normal or kurang manis.

Khabar anginRumours: eleven rare things to see in KL, new in 0.5.8

Percuma

Edisi lewat malamPercaya atau tidakTimes are KL game time

00:00–03:00 · Kampung Baru

Pontianak di bawah pokok pisang

A woman in white with long hair stands at the edge of a banana clump beside a kampung house, her back to you. Her laugh is loud when you're far off and faint when you're close, the way the old stories tell it. Get near, or look away for two seconds, and she's gone.

23:00–04:00 · an empty outer road

Perempuan berbaju putih minta tumpang

Drive a car down an empty road on the city's edge at night and she raises a hand at the kerb. You pass her. A few seconds later, check the back seat.

22:00–04:00 · a five-foot way

Toyol di kaki lima

Walk the shophouse arcades late and a small green bald child patters past and vanishes behind a pillar.

08:00–18:00 · a river bridge

Biawak melintas, trafik berhenti

A metre and a half of monitor lizard walks across where the road comes off the bridge, and the traffic waits. You can't run it over: it darts off first.

07:00–18:00 · Bukit Nanas, on foot

Monyet ragut beg plastik

Two or three macaques sit on the path up to KL Tower. Get close and one grabs a plastic bag and runs for the trees.

Ringkas

  • Pelangi07:00–18:30 · after rainA rainbow when a rain spell ends and the sun is low enough. Never at midday, when KL's sun is too high, just like the real thing.
  • Bunga api00:00 · over KLCCFireworks at midnight on some nights, and every night on New Year, Merdeka and Malaysia Day by your device's date, over Merdeka 118 too.
  • Tahi bintang21:00–05:00 · clear nightsA shooting star, about a second long, high over the city.
  • Wau16:00–18:30 · dry afternoonsWau bulan on long lines over the Dataran Merdeka padang or the Lake Gardens hill.
  • Monorel hantu02:30–03:30 · a monorail stationA lit, empty train glides through without stopping and without a sound. You can't board it.
  • UFO02:45–03:15 · between the towersA disc hovers between the Petronas tops, hums, then shoots straight up. The rarest of the eleven.

Nothing to collect, no counter and nothing saved. Each keeps its own hour, weather and place, and in free roam you might meet one an hour.
